Cooking

Gochujang-glazed chicken thighs

Worth learning here

Glazing late. Sugar burns long before chicken cooks, so the glaze goes on once the meat is nearly done.

What you need

  • 6 chicken thighs, bone in
  • 2 tbsp gochujang
  • 1 tbsp honey

Steps

7 steps
  1. 1Dry the thighs properly and salt them. Leave them out while the oven heats.Wet skin steams instead of browning.
  2. 2Roast skin up at 220°C for 25 minutes, no glaze yet.This is where the fat renders out.
