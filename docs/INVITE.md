# Inviting friends to Mise

Copy, swap in the link, send. The link unfurls into a card with Mise's face on it.

## Text to one friend

> I've been building a little cooking app called Mise. It helps you plan the week's dinners and shop without the wasted half-bunch of herbs. Would love you to try it: [your link]
>
> When it asks you to pick a plan, use code VIP26. On iPhone, add it to your Home Screen (Share, then Add to Home Screen) so it opens like an app. If anything feels off, tap Send feedback in the app and tell me.

## Group chat

> Hi all! I've been making a cooking app called Mise and I'd love a few of you to try it this week. You tell it who's eating and what you like, it helps you pick the week's dinners, builds a shopping list around what actually gets used up, and talks you through the cooking.
>
> [your link]
>
> - Use code VIP26 when it asks you to pick a plan.
> - On iPhone, add it to your Home Screen (Share, then Add to Home Screen).
> - Tap Send feedback in the app whenever something's off.
>
> What helps most:
> - Anywhere you got stuck or weren't sure what to tap
> - Dishes that didn't suit you, and why
> - Recipe steps that were unclear at the stove
> - Shopping list mistakes (wrong amounts, missing things)
> - Anything that looks broken on your phone (a screenshot is perfect)
>
> Thank you, honestly. Even "I opened it and didn't get it" is useful.
