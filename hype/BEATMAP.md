# Mise launch film — beat map (v4)

1920×1080 · 60 fps · 120 BPM · 104 beats · 52.0 s
Track: Mixkit #190 "Electro Dreams". Edit: 7.71–47.71 s (build → drop at beat 16 → the whole
drop section, uncut) + splice to 57.71–69.71 s (last breakdown bars → the return on beat 92), 2.5 s fade.
The demo keeps its own 40-beat clock; `WARP` in film.html stretches it (≈1.4× slower) and
leaves room for two full-frame cooking cuts. Rows below give film beats.
Beat b starts at t = b × 0.5 s.

**The idea:** Mise is an iOS app. A large iPhone 15 Pro on the right runs the app's real
flows, redrawn clean in its own type, colours and character. A big caption on the left
makes the film read with the sound off. Every feature shown exists in the app today (the
flows were captured from the running app with `capture.js` as reference): plan from the
fridge, the shopping list with "use first", asking Mise to change a recipe (three routes,
she rewrites it and updates the list), cook mode with timers, and asking her at the stove.

Brand: paper #FAF5F4, ink #221A15, brick #B44722, night #3C3F63 (the stove panel), Nunito
in the app, Geist for captions, the toque mark, and Mise's own avatar.
