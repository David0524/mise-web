## People cut-outs (assets/src/people/), added by the research agent

All files in people/ were downloaded on 2026-10-10 for a **private, non-public film** (the user gave permission to use any image found online). Public-domain and CC images are marked as such. Everything else is marked "unknown / private use only" and must not be published without clearance.

Files are downscaled to at most 4000 px on the long side. None of the people/ files carry watermarks.
